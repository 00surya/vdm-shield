A few things
worth knowing.

Where is my footage stored?+

On the computer running the VDM app. Camera footage, saved event clips and your learning files stay there. The website manages accounts, groups, licences, administration logs and device-health summaries. It does not host live video or saved footage.

Can I use cameras I already have?+

VDM works with compatible network camera streams, USB webcams and uploaded videos. Compatibility depends on your camera and connection. The current app supports up to four camera sources per running app, subject to your licence and hardware.

Does it learn automatically?+

Your team reviews examples and chooses when to start a learning update in the app. Updates are checked before they are used. More useful, correctly reviewed footage gives you more to learn from; improvement is not guaranteed simply by leaving a camera running. The detection features already included stay available as you teach VDM about your site.

Why does a possible-fight alert take time?+

VDM checks for sustained supporting evidence around the same two tracked people for at least five seconds before raising a possible-fight alert. Their movement, overlap and relative depth contribute to the check. Missing or conflicting evidence can restart it. This is a review aid, not proof of a fight: waving can still cause false alerts, and some incidents can be missed.

Will it catch every incident?+

No camera system can promise that. Lighting, distance, camera angle and obstructions all affect results. VDM helps you find moments to review; it can miss events and raise false alarms. A visible object alert does not confirm intent or whether an object is real. Test VDM in your own environment and keep people in the decision.

Does Eco mode turn off detection?+

Eco mode reduces how often the app checks a quiet live scene. Periodic checks continue, and movement brings normal processing back. You can enable it for each live camera. It is off by default, and saved videos use normal processing. Reduced checking can miss brief events; actual power savings depend on your hardware and scene.

Can multiple people use the same group?+

Yes. Invite multiple operators into a group and assign people to more than one group if needed. Operators activate the app for their assigned group and work with cameras on the device. Administrators manage organization access and licences on the website.

What do I need to get started?+

The downloadable preview is for Macs with Apple silicon. You’ll need an account, an available device licence and a compatible camera or saved video. Internet is required for activation and regular licence renewal. Performance depends on your Mac, the cameras you connect and the features you use.

Does VDM replace my video recorder?+

VDM is built for incident awareness and review. It saves available event clips and keeps a local event history. It does not provide continuous video recording, cloud video storage or remote live viewing from the website.
